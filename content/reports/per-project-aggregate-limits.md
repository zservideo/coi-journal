## What problem does a per project aggregate solve?

A general liability policy usually carries a per occurrence limit and a general aggregate, the most it will pay for most claims in a policy year. A trade working ten jobs has one aggregate across all ten. A large claim on someone else's project can use up limits you were counting on. The certificate shows the aggregate amount. It doesn't show how much of it is left.

## What does the CG 25 03 endorsement do?

ISO's CG 25 03, Designated Construction Project(s) General Aggregate Limit, gives each scheduled project its own general aggregate. Per [InsuranceXDate's summary](https://insurancexdate.com/insurance-forms/CG/CG-25-03/), each project aggregate equals the general aggregate shown in the declarations, and ongoing operations losses that can be attributed to a scheduled project reduce only that project's aggregate.

[IRMI describes the purpose](https://www.irmi.com/term/insurance-definitions/designated-construction-project-general-aggregate-limit-endorsement) plainly: the endorsement enables a contractor to meet contract requirements to keep a specified amount of insurance for a specific project. ISO's companion form, [CG 25 04](https://insurancexdate.com/insurance-forms/CG/CG-25-04/), does the same for designated locations rather than projects.

## What doesn't CG 25 03 change?

- **[Completed operations](/dictionary/completed-operations/).** Per IRMI, the endorsement doesn't affect the policy's other limits, including the products-completed operations aggregate. Claims after your job is finished still draw from a shared pool.
- **The per occurrence limit.** Unchanged.
- **Unscheduled projects.** The form applies to designated projects. If yours isn't designated, it isn't covered by the separate aggregate.

Table: What CG 25 03 changes on a trade's general liability policy
| Limit | Without CG 25 03 | With CG 25 03 |
|---|---|---|
| General aggregate, ongoing operations | One aggregate shared across every job | A separate aggregate for each scheduled project, equal to the declarations amount |
| Products-completed operations aggregate | Shared | Still shared |
| Per occurrence limit | As declared | Unchanged |
| Projects not on the schedule | Share the policy aggregate | Still share the policy aggregate |

## Why do project requirements ask for a per project aggregate?

Owners, public agencies especially, often require the trade's aggregate to apply per project. A GC passing that requirement down needs proof that it applies to this project, not just that the trade owns some version of the endorsement. See [per project aggregate](/dictionary/per-project-aggregate/) in the Dictionary and our paper on [tracking per project requirements](/reports/per-project-requirements-and-renewals/).

## How does a GC check a per project aggregate?

1. **Ask for the endorsement**, not just the [certificate](/dictionary/certificate-of-insurance/).
2. **Find your project on the schedule**, by the same name and address as the subcontract, or confirm the carrier's wording applies to all projects.
3. **Check the completed operations requirement separately.** If the owner wants dedicated completed operations limits, CG 25 03 alone won't meet it.
4. **Re-check at renewal.** A new policy may carry a different schedule.

A per project aggregate is one of the cheaper protections in a trade's program. It's also one of the easiest to claim on a certificate without actually having it for your job.
