## Why payment is the lever

A deficiency notice asks a trade to call its broker. A held payment makes the trade call today. EPIC's 2026 guidance on trade risk recommends contract provisions that allow payment to be withheld when insurance coverage is incomplete or misaligned with the contract.

## Put it in the contract

The control is only as good as the subcontract language behind it. The pieces to cover with counsel:

- **The condition.** Current certificates and endorsements meeting the project requirements are a condition of payment.
- **The scope.** Whether a gap holds the whole payment or only part of it.
- **The cure.** How a trade fixes it, and how fast payment follows.
- **The law.** State prompt payment statutes and public contract rules may limit what you can hold and for how long. This is where counsel earns their fee.

## Build it into the monthly cycle

1. **Before each pay application is approved,** the compliance desk confirms the trade's file is current: no expired policies, no open deficiencies past their due date.
2. **If it isn't,** the PM gets a short note: which trade, what's missing, since when.
3. **The trade gets notice** with the pay app, naming the specific item and the fix.
4. **Payment releases** when the item clears, on the next cycle or sooner if your process allows.

The point is to make compliance a routine step, not an escalation. When it's routine, it's not personal.

## Keep it fair

- **Same rule for every trade.** Announce it at award, in writing.
- **Specific notices.** "Missing CG 20 37 for policy GL-123, expired 10/1" gets fixed. "Insurance noncompliant" gets an argument.
- **Fast release.** Hold no longer than the gap lasts.

## Related controls

IRMI's guidance on struggling trades lists other payment-side tools, including joint checks, payroll support, direct purchase of materials and more frequent pay applications. Those address financial risk. Insurance compliance is a separate condition and works best kept separate.

Pair this with a [defensible COI file](/reports/defensible-coi-file/), so every hold has a documented reason.
