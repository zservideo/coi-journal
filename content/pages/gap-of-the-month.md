## How it works

1. Tell us what happened in a few sentences. Please don't send names, policy numbers or documents.
2. If we pick your story, we'll write back before anything runs.
3. We strip out anything that could identify a company or person, and we credit you only if you ask us to.

## When picks run

The first Gap of the Month runs in The COI Digest in December, with the launch. Until then, [Spot the Gap](/spot-the-gap/) shows the kinds of gaps we cover, on specimen certificates.

## What makes a good submission

- A gap you found, or one that cost you: a missing endorsement, a short limit, a lapsed policy, a wrong name.
- When it was found: at onboarding, mid-project, or after a claim.
- What fixed it, if anything did.
