About COI Journal

A publication about construction insurance compliance, for the people who check, issue and rely on contractor certificates of insurance.

What COI Journal is

COI Journal covers construction insurance compliance. That means contractor certificates of insurance, the endorsements behind them, the project requirements they're measured against, and what happens when coverage turns out not to be there.

We write for general contractors, the brokers who insure their trade contractors, project owners and the risk and compliance people in between.

Who publishes it

COI Journal is published by Forsured. Forsured makes software that checks contractor COIs and endorsements against project requirements.

We publish COI Journal because the category has almost no reporting of its own, and because we learn a lot about where coverage breaks. Sharing what we learn, with our ownership in plain view, is better for everyone than another vendor blog.

How we keep it separate

  • No Forsured company news. That lives on forsured.ai.
  • No rankings, grades or reviews of software vendors, including Forsured.
  • Every fact we didn't produce ourselves is cited.
  • Contributors who have a business relationship with Forsured say so in their byline.

The full rules are in our publishing principles.
