# Why we're starting COI Journal

Contractor certificates of insurance decide who pays when a job goes wrong. Almost nobody reports on them. We're going to.

By The editors, COI Journal. November 13, 2026. Published by Forsured.

## The short version

- COI Journal covers construction insurance compliance: contractor COIs, endorsements, project requirements and what happens when coverage fails.
- It is published by Forsured, which makes COI compliance software. We say so on every page and every email.
- We don't rank or review software vendors, including Forsured. Original reporting, public records and research only.
- The weekly email, The COI Digest, goes out Tuesday mornings.

Every construction project runs on a stack of paper that most people only read when something breaks. The certificate of insurance says a contractor is covered. The endorsements say who else is covered and how. The project requirements say what everyone agreed to. When those three don't line up, the gap shows up later, usually after an injury, a claim or a denied tender.

That gap is our beat.

## What we'll publish

- **The news desk.** Carrier, state and court changes that change what a general contractor or broker has to check.
- **COI 101.** Short explainers on one term at a time: additional insured, primary and noncontributory, waiver of subrogation, per project aggregate.
- **COI Watch.** A running log of rating changes, new endorsement forms, rule changes and large project awards.
- **The Claim File.** One case a month from public records: what the COI said, what the policy said, and who paid.
- **The COI Gap Index.** Our flagship research: what share of contractor COIs fail project requirements, and why. It launches once the sample is big enough to be honest. Our [methodology](https://coijournal.com/methodology/) explains the rules.
