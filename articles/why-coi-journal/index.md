# Why we're starting COI Journal

Contractor certificates of insurance decide who pays when a job goes wrong. Almost nobody reports on them. We're going to.

By Zach Servideo, COI Journal. November 13, 2026. Published by Forsured.

## The short version

- COI Journal covers construction insurance compliance: contractor COIs, endorsements, project requirements and what happens when coverage fails.
- It is published by Forsured, which makes COI compliance software. We say so on every page and every email.
- We don't rank or review software vendors, including Forsured. Original reporting, public records and research only.
- The weekly email, The COI Digest, goes out Tuesday mornings.

Every construction project runs on a stack of paper that most people only read when something breaks. The certificate of insurance says a contractor is covered. The endorsements say who else is covered and how. The project requirements say what everyone agreed to. When those three don't line up, the gap shows up later, usually after an injury, a claim or a denied tender.

That gap is our beat.

## What we'll publish

- **[Reports](https://coijournal.com/reports/).** Analyst reports and white papers: long-form, sourced analysis of where contractor COIs break and the checks that catch it.
- **[The Resource Center](https://coijournal.com/resources/).** Everything we publish to keep, in one place, including the COI Gap Index, our flagship research on what share of contractor COIs fail project requirements and why. It launches once the sample is big enough to be honest; our [methodology](https://coijournal.com/methodology/) explains the rules. Until then, **The Number** runs one sourced figure at a time, with the arithmetic behind it and our read on who produced it.
- **[The COI Dictionary](https://coijournal.com/dictionary/).** One term at a time, with the form numbers: additional insured, primary and noncontributory, waiver of subrogation, per project aggregate.
- **[Spot the Gap](https://coijournal.com/spot-the-gap/).** A specimen certificate next to a set of project requirements. Find what fails before we tell you.
- **[Worth Reading](https://coijournal.com/worth-reading/).** The best construction insurance reporting from other outlets each week, summarized in a few lines with a link to the full story.
- **Plain Speak.** Short videos with [Steve Massei](https://coijournal.com/people/steve-massei/), a general contractor who explains one insurance idea at a time in plain English.
