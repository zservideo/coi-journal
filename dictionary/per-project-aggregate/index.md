# Per project aggregate

COI Journal, The COI Dictionary. Published by Forsured. Reference only, not legal or insurance advice.

Forms: CG 25 03

## Definition

An endorsement that gives each construction project its own general aggregate limit, so claims on one job can't use up the limit for yours.

## On the certificate

The general aggregate line has boxes for policy, project and location. The project box should be checked.

## What to check

The project box and an attached endorsement such as CG 25 03, the Designated Construction Project(s) General Aggregate Limit.

## The common gap

A per policy aggregate shared across every job the contractor runs. A busy year elsewhere can leave little of the limit for your project.
